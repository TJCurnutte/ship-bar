ship at 9.7, not when it looks done

A free skill that has 20 reviewer agents score every AI-built page, so nothing ships until the median hits 9.7.

1 · build An agent builds the page. This one worked, but still felt a tad vibe coded.

2 · screenshots and checks Capture 1440 and 390, run Lighthouse, check the console, overflow and a banned-style scan.

3 · 20 reviewers score Twenty narrow-lens reviewers each score out of 10 and flag blockers.

4 · median 9.7 or no ship The score is the median. Under 9.7, or any blocker, it doesn't ship.

5 · fix and rerun Fix the top-ranked issues, rebuild, score again from scratch. v13 slipped to 9.3.

6 · ship Clear the bar and ship. v12 passed at 9.7.

questions

What is ship bar?

A free skill for AI agents that build websites. Twenty reviewer agents score each build from screenshots and checks, and it only ships at a median of 9.7 out of 10 with no blockers.

How do I use it?

Download skill.md, add it to your agent's skills or instructions, and run the reviewers on every build.

Where are the full details?

In the white paper: the method steps, every check and threshold, the 20 reviewers, and this site's own scores.
